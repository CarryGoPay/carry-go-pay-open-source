Carry Go Pay Open Source
Simplifying Cross-Border Payments for Africa

Carry Go Pay is a digital financial technology platform focused on making cross-border payments simpler, more accessible, and more transparent for individuals, merchants, SMEs, and businesses.

This repository contains open-source components, documentation, examples, and technical resources related to the Carry Go Pay platform and its mission to improve access to digital cross-border payment services.

Important: Carry Go Pay is designed to work with regulated financial partners. This open-source repository does not provide banking, payment, foreign-exchange, custody, settlement, or regulated financial services by itself.

Our Mission

We help African businesses and individuals sending money across borders solve slow, expensive, and complicated cross-border payments by connecting them to a seamless multi-currency payment and payout experience through regulated financial partners, so they can move money across borders quickly and confidently.

The Problem

Individuals and businesses operating across African borders can face:

High cross-border transaction costs
Slow payment processes
Complicated payment workflows
Limited access to convenient multi-currency services
Difficulty paying suppliers, staff, and business partners internationally
Limited access to understandable digital payment tools

These barriers can make it harder for individuals and businesses to participate in regional and global commerce.

The Solution

Carry Go Pay provides a digital experience that helps customers access and manage cross-border payment services through regulated financial partners.

The platform focuses on:

Digital customer onboarding
Cross-border payment initiation
Multi-currency payment experiences
Transaction information and transparency
Partner integrations through APIs
Customer education and support
Agent-assisted onboarding for underserved users

Carry Go Pay does not hold or store customer funds. Under the partner model, regulated financial partners provide the underlying payment, foreign-exchange, settlement, and payout services.
